## Geopolitical Risk and Crude Oil Market Analysis (2010-2026)

Analyzing the impact of 25 major geopolitical events on WTI and Brent crude oil prices using XGBoost, event impact quantification, and correlation analysis across 4,047 trading days.
## Key Findings
    •	XGBoost achieved $1.25 MAE and 95.5% R2 on unseen test data. 
	•	7 day overreaction window identified as potential trading signal
	•	Markets stabilize within 60 days of most geopolitical shocks
	•	Geopolitical risk better used as risk flag than price predictor
## Tools Used
Python, XGBoost, Pandas, Seaborn, Matplotlib, Google Colab
## Dataset
Kaggle: Global Oil Prices and Geopolitical Events (2010-2026) by kavyadhyani
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/kavyadhyani/global-oil-prices-and-geopolitical-events
